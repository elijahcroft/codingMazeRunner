# Strange Planet: new skins and things to earn

A plan for the garage's next items: stuff young kids really want (creeper,
dinos, ninjas, monsters, lightning, sparkly things) and new ways to earn them,
so the kids who earn fewer stars still have something to chase.

Branch: `planet-skins` (worktree `/home/ej/pq-planet-skins`), cut from
`strange-planet` at 7615d5a, while other work goes on in the main checkout.

## Where the garage stands

Before this branch: 105 items in 8 slots (paint, topper, trail, world, dance,
glow, pet, horn). Star unlocks run all the way up to 150 ★, and there are 53
sites. The younger easy-mode class gets 1 ★ per practice site, so it ends up
around 40 ★, and finds no mystery gifts, because gifts only appear in zones
1–15. Most of what's new should therefore unlock in other ways than more stars.

Rules the garage already keeps, and the new items keep too:

- Nothing is random and nothing is taken away. No loot boxes: duplicates and
  near misses feel bad, especially for some of our students.
- Calm mode and the projector draw at t = 0, so every animation must look
  right standing still.
- Item keys are unique across all slots.
- No brand names on the badge or shop text. Creeper and Crewmate are the
  characters' own names, so they stay.

## Batch 4: built (October 29): ideas 1, 2, 4, 5, 7 and 8 below

### 🎨 The Paint Shop
A button at the top of the garage's Paint tab. The student writes code with
`paint(x, y, "red")` and `fill("navy")`, and it paints a 10 × 7 grid of the
rover's body, seen from above with the front on the right, the way the rover
starts on a map: x runs 1 to 10 back to front, y 1 to 7 top to bottom.

- The program runs on the server in the same safe interpreter as missions
  (`planet.Painter`, a `Rover` with an `Easel` for a world), so the whole of
  the class's Python works: loops, `if`, `def`, variables, `%`. The rover's
  commands and sensors say they're for missions.
- The squares go on one by one in the order the code painted them, with a
  little note each, so a loop is something you watch working. It repaints
  by itself half a second after typing stops; a typo mid-word keeps the last
  painting up.
- Clicking a square adds `paint(x, y, "colour")` for it, in the colour last
  pressed under the code, so younger kids start by clicking and read the code
  it makes. Colour names in the code show in their own colour. Six examples
  to load: One square, A stripe, Racing stripes, Checkers (nested loops and
  an `if`), Rainbow (a `def` with two values) and Smiley.
- Mistakes get the planet's kind of message, like "x 0 is off the rover: x
  goes from 1 to 10. range(10) counts 0 to 9: range(1, 11) counts 1 to 10."
- **✓ Wear it** keeps the paint as 70 letters in `look["art"]` with the paint
  `mine` (the garage's "My paint", legendary), so it's on the map, the
  replays, the teacher's page and the class screen. Painting with a loop
  earns the 🎨 **Pixel Artist** badge, and the projector's feed says who
  painted their rover.
- **🧽 Wipe paint** on the teacher's page takes a painted rover back to
  Moonstone, if one isn't right for the class screen.

## Batch 2: the ideas list (1, 2, 4, 5, 7 and 8 are built, see Batch 4)

Roughly the most kid excitement for the least work first.

1. **🎨 Paint Shop: code your own skin.** A site where the code *is* the skin:
   `paint(x, y, "green")` on a 10 × 7 grid (the same grid `pixelArt` already
   uses). Loops make stripes, nested loops make checkers, and an `if` makes a
   face. The result becomes the student's own paint, saved as 70 colour
   letters. It teaches loops better than any star, and kids love designing
   skins. The teacher can hide one from the projector.
2. **🛞 Wheels slot:** monster-truck tyres, flame rims, neon wheels that glow,
   hover pads (no wheels, it floats), tank treads. A new slot draws right over
   the six wheel rectangles in `drawRover`.
3. **🤖 Mecha, a paint that levels up:** Mk I at 60 ★, Mk II at 110 ★ (bigger
   armour), Mk III at 155 ★ (glowing visor and jets). One item whose drawing
   reads the student's stars (the look sent to the page and the projector would
   carry a `mk`).
4. **🧩 Set bonuses:** wear every piece of a pack and get something extra, like
   the creeper face blinking, pickups shooting green confetti, or a skeleton's
   eyes glowing. The garage shows "3 / 4 Blocky Boom" so kids chase full sets.
5. **🎁 Gifts in the practice zones**, for the easy-mode class: one per practice
   zone, handing out early pieces (Creeper face sticker, Bat, Bubbles). This
   needs the practice zones, which are still uncommitted in the main checkout.
6. **🏷 Name plate:** a plate on the back of the rover showing a word picked
   from a fixed list (GG, Pro, Boss, 🔥, 😎, Turbo…). There's no free text, so
   nothing needs moderating.
7. **🎉 Pick 1 of 3 at every rank-up:** choose one of three locked items to
   unlock right now. It's a surprise without being random about whether you win.
8. **🏅 Zone master legendaries:** each zone's Master badge gives a themed
   legendary: Ice → Frost Crown, Wind → Tornado Cape, Lasers → Laser Eyes,
   Cargo → Gold Truck paint.
9. **❄️ Seasonal drops that stay forever:** a winter pack (Santa hat, snowman
   pet, candy-cane trail, Snow Globe world), Valentine's (heart glasses),
   spring (bee pet). They're released on a date and earnable from then on,
   never time-limited.
10. **🗳 Class vote:** the projector offers 3 designs for the next pack, kids
    vote on their screens, and the winner ships next week.
11. **More kid favourites to draw** (Banana and Shark Bite are in Batch 3): Lava Golem, Galaxy Dragon pet, Robot
    Butler pet, Gamer Headset topper, Diamond Armor paint, Gold Chain topper,
    Rocket Boots glow, Confetti Cannon dance, Floss and Dab style dances (the
    rover wiggles side to side), Banana paint, Donut topper, Shark Bite paint
    with teeth on the front, Lava Lamp world.
12. **Teacher's call, silly sounds:** a whoopee-cushion horn and a rubber-chicken
    horn. Kids 8–10 would love them, but they might be too much in class. They
    could sit behind a teacher switch.

## Merging back

The other sessions have big uncommitted changes to `planet.html`, `server.py`
and `planet_render.js`. To keep the merge easy, this branch:

- appends new paints, pets, glows and toppers at the **end** of
  `planet_render.js` (`Object.assign` plus a `TOPPERS` table that `drawTopper`
  checks first), not inside the existing objects;
- adds the new items as one block at the end of `GARAGE`, and the new badges as
  one block after the coding badges;
- in `planet.html`, adds new `else if` branches at the ends of `drawTrail` and
  `dance`, new entries at the ends of `WORLDS`, the horn table and
  `HORN_ICONS`/`DANCE_ICONS`, and the dance's `alpha`, `flash` and `puff` in
  the two places a dance is drawn.

If a hunk does conflict, keep both sides: every change here only adds items.
