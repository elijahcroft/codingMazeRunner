How Strange Planet works

  1. Your program drives the rover.Commands run in order, top to bottom. Every command takes one tick of planet time. 
    move(3)        turn_left()
turn_right()   wait()
  2. Every zone has a hidden rule.Nobody will tell you what it is. The first site of each zone is where you notice it. Your code from the last site is loaded, so predict first: will it still work here?
  3. Experiment like a scientist.Run, watch the replay, step through it one tick at a time, and read the output. print() shows you numbers as the rover drives. Write your best guess in the Field journal.
