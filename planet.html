More tips
      
The editor helps you type: start a word and it suggests commands (Enter or Tab picks one), brackets and quotes close themselves, and a slip gets a ⚠ on its line. Point at a word to see what it does. Ctrl+/ turns lines into notes.

      
Earn every star you can on each site. Stuck? 🕹 Scout drives by hand, 📻 Hints asks mission control. Made a good def? 🧰 Functions keeps it for later sites.

      
Stars unlock paint, hats, trails and new looks for the whole map in the 🔧 Garage at the top. Spot a 🎁 on the map? Drive over it for a secret one. Click your rover to say hi.
